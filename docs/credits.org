* Design presets and variants (docs/components.org, "Presets and the picker")

The presets and variants study real sites for colour values, font names and arrangement only. No code, photos, words or logos come from them. Licensed faces on those sites are replaced by open faces under the SIL Open Font License, served by Bunny Fonts: Manrope for Satoshi, Inter for Helvetica Neue, and PT Serif and Work Sans as used. Tailwind Plus, Relume, Flowbite Blocks and Preline are studied for taxonomy only; no code from them is used.

These MIT-licensed projects supplied scales, names or markup shapes. The CSS is written for libresite on its own tokens.

| Project | Copyright | What we took |
|---------+-----------+--------------|
| Radix Themes | Copyright (c) 2023 WorkOS | the space steps (4 to 64 px) and type steps (12 to 60 px) of =--space-1..9= and =--text-1..9= |
| daisyUI | Copyright (c) 2020 Pouya Saadeghi | the split into control, box and badge radii, and the =--depth= switch |
| shadcn/ui | Copyright (c) 2023 shadcn | colour roles as ground and text pairs (=primary= and =primary_fg=) |
