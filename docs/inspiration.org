* Why it exists

The variety work made every site different, but not striking: the ten fixture sites still read as one tidy template in different clothes. This file is the harvest for the next step. It lists the ideas that made surveyed sites memorable, each in one line a developer can rebuild from, with where it was seen and whether its code may be reused here.

Nothing in the library changed with this file. The pieces chosen from it are built in the component library, with credit recorded in =docs/credits.org= (started when the first copied piece lands) for anything copied.

* How to read an entry

Each entry is one line: the idea, what makes it striking, then its source, the source's licence and one of two words.

- *copy*: the source is MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, CC0, the Unlicense or CC-BY, so its code may be adapted here, with the source URL and licence recorded in =docs/credits.org= (and visible credit for CC-BY). Photos and illustrations shipped inside a template often carry their own licence; check each one before reusing it, and prefer drawing our own.
- *study only*: proprietary, GPL or unlicensed. Look, learn the pattern, rebuild it from scratch in our own code and art; copy nothing (no markup, CSS, scripts, images or wording).

Fonts named here are all open-source (SIL Open Font License or Apache-2.0), so the fonts themselves are *copy* even when the layout that inspired the pairing is *study only*. Examples in quotes are our own invented wording for a trade, not the source's text.

* The survey

631 sites screenshotted on 2026-10-20 (649 listed, 18 failed to load), each at 1440 wide (first screen and full page) and 390 wide (phone). The screenshots are kept privately, out of git.

- Open-source and free templates (182, reusable): HTML5 UP (44, CC-BY-3.0), Start Bootstrap (20, MIT), Astro themes with a permissive licence (118: MIT 112, CC-BY 2, Apache-2.0, BSD, CC0, Unlicense).
- Astro themes without a usable licence (80: none, GPL or other), study only.
- Webflow template marketplace (195: 77 trade templates for plumbers, roofers, cleaners, landscapers, HVAC, builders and movers, and 118 featured templates), study only.
- Framer template marketplace (116), study only.
- Awwwards sites of the day (76), study only.

Every page was read from its full-page screenshot; 1,150 one-line ideas were logged (215 openings, 489 sections, 111 type, 104 colour, 83 art, 71 navigation, 37 motion, the last from reading the source of 64 animated sites). What follows is the best of them, chosen for what would make a trade or local-business owner say "that's mine".

What the survey shows, in short:

1. The striking trade templates do not decorate more; they commit harder. One huge word, one saturated ground, one photo treatment, carried through every section.
2. The best sections answer an owner's real question with a form a visitor can touch: a price estimate, a problem picker, a before/after slider, a "what to expect" card, a live job board.
3. Type does most of the work: very large, very condensed or very light, with one word switched to italic serif, script or a colour.
4. Proof sits next to the action: rating, licence, years and response time sit in the header, the hero or the button itself, not in a section further down.
5. Motion is small and purposeful (a ticker of promises, a count-up, a wipe from before to after), and the good ones stop cleanly under reduced motion.
